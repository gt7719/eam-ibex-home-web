# Version 76

- Combined partner organizations and project team under the permission-aware **Project implementers** admin tab while preserving their separate data, controls and permissions.
- Reorganized administrator access settings into readable menu-aligned groups without changing any server permission identifiers.
- Added **Users**, **Verification settings** and **Delivery history** areas to Website users.
- Added non-secret email, SMS and Turnstile readiness reporting plus masked delivery audit history.
- Prevented administrators from requiring email or phone verification until the corresponding delivery service is ready.
- Standardized day-mode permission-card contrast, two-column desktop layout and one-column mobile behavior.
